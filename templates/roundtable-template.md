# Panel Roundtable — Template (copy for each topic)

One question, one document, one independent answer per seat — then a
synthesis and a decision.

## How to use this template

1. Copy this document and rename it: "Roundtable: [Topic] — YYYY-MM-DD".
2. Fill in the Question and Context / Constraints below. Keep the
   question verbatim.
3. The coordinator writes its own answer section first, so the other
   seats are answering the same question and context.
4. The owner pastes the copy-paste prompt (below) plus the document
   link to each other seat.
5. The coordinator synthesizes once all answers are in.
6. The owner records the Decision at the end of this document.

## Instructions for panelists

- Write ONLY in your own section below. Do not edit, move, or overwrite
  anyone else's section.
- Include all of the following in your answer: your recommendation,
  your reasoning, the risks, what you would change about the framing
  of the question, and your confidence.
- Do not include credentials, codes, or full account numbers in your
  answer.
- If you cannot edit this document directly, your answer will be pasted
  in by the owner or the coordinator and marked as pasted. That is
  expected for some seats — see the note at the end of this document.

### Answer format

- **Recommendation:** your answer in one or two sentences.
- **Reasoning:** why you recommend it.
- **Risks:** what could go wrong, and what to watch for.
- **What I would change about the framing:** anything in the question
  or context you would reframe, add, or challenge.
- **Confidence:** high / medium / low, and what would change it.

## Note: direct edit vs. paste-back

This document is the source of truth. Direct editing is preferred, and
paste-back is the fallback — both end in the same place.

- Some seats can usually edit a shared document directly from a link.
- Some seats cannot edit documents in place at all (a tool limitation);
  their answers will normally be pasted in.
- Some seats are hit-or-miss from a pasted link alone.

When an answer is pasted in, mark it as pasted and keep the wording
verbatim.
