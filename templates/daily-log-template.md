# Daily Log — Template

Derived from the Log Standard (see
`../log-standard/AI-Assistant-Log-Standard.md`, sections 4, 6, and 10).
One document per seat per month, titled `[Seat] Daily Log — YYYY-MM`.
Start a new document on the first day of each month and link the
previous month in the header.

```
[Seat] Daily Log — YYYY-MM
Header:
- Covers: [what this log covers — first-hand sessions with the owner, month and year].
- Sources / channels: [the sources and channels entries draw on].
- Updated: [how the log is actually updated — by the seat itself on a schedule, proven by a dated entry; or prompted or pasted by the owner. Do not claim "automated / daily / synced" until one unattended write is proven and logged Completed with a Verified tag.]

[Weekday, Month D, YYYY]

[TOPIC HEADING IN CAPITALS]
1. Label: One line — outcome first, then the detail someone would need to verify or redo it. (time, channel). [Evidence tag]

For Current State: [Add / Change / Close proposals, each citing its entry number — or "None".]

Footer: Entries: [count]. Coverage: [sources checked; known gaps; no entry does not mean no activity]. Exclusions applied: no credentials, codes, or full account numbers; no health or legal identifiers (those live in the Private Notes companion document).
Optional Usage line: [only if the seat's platform exposes usage, marked as approximate; never invent a figure.]
```

## The rules baked into this skeleton

- **Header is three lines:** what the log covers; the sources and
  channels it draws on; how it is updated, stated honestly.
- **Order:** newest day at the bottom. Nothing sits above the first day
  except the header — no standing summaries, snapshots, or open-item
  lists at the top. A list at the top goes stale and is the first thing
  other seats copy.
- **Day heading:** full date with weekday. Check the weekday against a
  calendar before writing it.
- **Topic headings:** short, in capitals, one per subject. Reuse the
  same heading on later days so a topic can be followed.
- **Entries** are numbered from 1 each day, so any entry can be cited
  as "Seat 2, Oct 9, #20". No entry for a day with no activity;
  scheduled checks that found nothing get one summary line, not one
  line per run.
- **Entry shape (Full tier), exactly:**
  `N. Label: What happened, in one line. (time, channel). [Evidence]`
- **Labels:** Discussed, Decided (add "Standing" if it applies from
  now on), Completed (needs an evidence tag), Open (with who holds the
  next step: Awaiting the owner, Pending, or Blocked — and on what),
  Handoff ("From → To, the question, status"), Corrected, Disputed
  (citing the other log's entry number and your evidence).
- **Evidence tags:** `[Verified: how]`, `[Owner said]`, `[Unverified]`,
  `[Inferred: from what]`. "Resolved", "fixed", and "sent" need a
  Verified tag; without one, write what was tried and mark it Open.
- **Corrections are appended, never rewritten.** A Corrected entry
  names which entry was wrong, what it said, what is true, and how the
  error was found.
- **First-hand only.** The log holds what the seat did, saw itself, or
  was told directly by the owner in its own session — never open items,
  preferences, completed actions, or facts copied from another seat's
  log. Summaries of other logs belong in a Cross-Read document, with a
  source on every line.
- **Log as you go.** Append entries as work happens; close the day by
  the end of the day in the owner's timezone.
