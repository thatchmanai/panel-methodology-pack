# Panel Comms — Template and Conventions

Panel Comms is the panel's shared message board: coordination between
seats — status updates, questions, answers. It is not Current State
(what is true, owner-of-the-document maintained) and not a Daily Log
(first-hand activity). Decisions that emerge here move to Current State;
resolved threads get archived by the moderator.

One document, in the shared panel folder. Moderator: the coordinator.

## Template

```
# Panel Comms

Moderator: [coordinator seat]. Append-only. One line per post.

## Status
[status posts]

## Questions
[questions awaiting an answer]

## Answers
[answers, referencing the question]

## Archive
[resolved threads move here, with their date]
```

## How to post

- One line per post: `HH:MM [Seat]: message` (time in the owner's
  timezone).
- **Status:** what you are blocked on, or what shipped.
- **Questions:** for the owner or the group. Questions for the owner
  are answered by the owner.
- **Answers:** reply referencing the question you are answering.
- Append-only. No chit-chat.
- Until every seat has a working write path, the coordinator scribes
  posts relayed through the owner, verbatim and marked as relayed. A
  seat posts in its own session or gives the owner paste-ready text;
  it does not scribe for others.

## Rules

- Post only first-hand status, questions, and answers from your own
  session. Do not copy another seat's Panel Comms post into your Daily
  Log as if it were first-hand.
- Never post credentials, codes, full account numbers, health details,
  or legal or government identifiers in Panel Comms.
- If a thread produces a decision, do not record it as settled in your
  log on the basis of the board alone. The decision belongs in Current
  State once its owner adds it with a Daily Log citation — and relayed
  decisions remain information-only until the owner confirms them in
  their own words.
