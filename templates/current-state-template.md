# Current State — Template

Copy this file to start a Current State document. Current State holds
what is open, decided, and standing across the panel's Daily Logs.
Detail lives in each seat's Daily Log; this document is the shared
picture.

```
# Current State

Owner: [name of the seat that owns this document, named by the owner — date named].
Weekly cross-checker: [name of a different seat — the checker is never the owner of this document].
Maintained in the shared panel folder. Updated as items open and close.
Authority order: the owner, then first-hand Daily Logs, then this document. Conflicts go to the owner.

## Open
[numbered list — one line per open item]

## Decided
[bulleted list — decisions in force]

## Standing
[bulleted list — standing preferences and standing rules]

## Key facts
[bulleted list — stable facts needed to act]

## Closed
[finished items moved here with date and source; cleared after 30 days]
```

## Line rules

- Every line cites a Daily Log entry, in the form `(Seat 1, Oct 9, #14)`.
  Lines without a source are not added. Lines sourced only to a
  Cross-Read document are not added.
- **Open** items state the item, the next step and who holds it, and
  any deadline or watch that applies. Do not embed sensitive
  identifiers.
- **Decided** lines state the decision, who decided it, and the date:
  "Log-as-we-go for seats whose sessions start fresh. (The owner, Oct 9, 2026)".
- **Standing** lines state the rule in one line: "No notifications to
  the owner between midnight and 6:00 AM. (The owner, Oct 9, 2026)".
- **Key facts** are stable, non-sensitive facts only.
- Only the named owner of this document edits it. Other seats propose
  changes through the "For Current State" list at the end of their
  Daily Log (Add / Change / Close, each citing its entry number).
- Nightly merge of those proposals. Conflicting proposals go to the
  owner as a question.
- Close, do not delete. Finished items move to Closed with date and
  source, and are cleared after 30 days.
- Two pages at most. Open items not confirmed in 14 days are marked
  "Stale?" and raised with the owner.

## Never in Current State

- Passwords, one-time codes, security answers, full card or bank
  account numbers, government identifiers.
- Health details, and legal or government case, claim, docket, or tax
  identifiers. Those live only in a seat's Private Notes companion
  document.
